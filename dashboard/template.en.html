Detecting in real time who is working without a helmet or vest

A personal protective equipment (PPE) detector trained on real construction-site images. It identifies helmets, vests, gloves, goggles and boots, and flags when they are missing, to raise safety alerts from the cameras already on site.

How it was built

The same process as in a pilot with site cameras.

Data1,000 construction-site images labelled with 12 classes: PPE worn, PPE missing, people and forklifts.
ModelPretrained YOLO11n fine-tuned for PPE. Lightweight on purpose: it runs on CPU, no expensive servers.
EvaluationPer-class metrics and, above all, the per-image alert, which is what safety teams actually use.
DeploymentOn-site RTSP cameras → local detector → alerts and incident log, without sending video to third parties.
